# Artifact Sprite Descriptions — New Artifacts (Phase 06)

All 16 new artifacts use `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/artifact_placeholder.png` as a working placeholder until their sprites are provided. Place the finished sprites at the paths listed below.

Sprite convention:
- `_sprite` = small inventory icon (~64×64 px), isometric top-down angle
- `_spriteFull` = large dossier view (~256×256 px), detailed illustration with background

## Custodian Order

### `overseers_brass_helm`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/overseers_brass_helm.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A full-coverage Victorian-era diving helmet repurposed for land use. Made of riveted brass, with a large circular viewport flanked by two rotating lens arrays on pivot mounts. Aural amplifier funnels extend from the sides like brass ears. Surface is engraved with Custodian owl motifs and gear-tooth borders. Slight patina of verdigris around the rivets.

---

### `custodian_sight_array`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/custodian_sight_array.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A goggles-style device with three independent lens housings arranged horizontally, mounted on a jointed brass armature that wraps around the head. The lenses are different tints: clear/magnification, amber/thermal, and violet/spectral. Each rotates independently on spring-loaded bearings. Tiny gear teeth are visible on the rotating ring of each lens.

---

### `custodian_calibration_gauntlets`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/custodian_calibration_gauntlets.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** Elbow-length brass gauntlets with articulated finger joints. Each knuckle has a small recessed dial with tick marks. The back of the hand displays a torque readout in the form of a radial gauge. The cuffs flare slightly and are engraved with the Custodian crest (an owl clutching a gear). The inner lining is padded brown leather.

---

### `custodian_iron_sabatons`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/custodian_iron_sabatons.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** Full-plate iron sabatons (armoured foot covers) with an articulated toe section. The unique feature: a visible spring coil at the heel, compressed under a mushroom-shaped dampener pad. The front is engraved with subtle gear-track patterns. Despite their military look, the toe tips have rubber dampener pads indicating their stealth function.

---

### `custodian_winding_key`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/custodian_winding_key.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** An oversized clockwork winding key, roughly 30 cm long. The bow (grip end) is cast in the shape of a stylised owl head with garnet eyes. The shank is faceted brass with a subtle spiral groove. The bit (tip) is a square cross-section that appears to morph slightly depending on viewing angle, as if it subtly reshapes itself. A faint glow emanates from the tip.

## House Blackwater

### `blackwater_flood_hood`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/blackwater_flood_hood.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A riveted leather diving hood styled like a 19th-century deep-sea diver's head covering, but for a human-sized wearer. The exterior is coated in thick black tar/pitch, giving it a matte, almost organic look. Rivets run along the seams in two rows. A small round viewport window is set into the front, sealed with rubber gasket. Waterline stains and salt deposits encrust the lower half.

---

### `alchemical_rebreather`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/alchemical_rebreather.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A face-mounted apparatus in the style of a steampunk gas mask crossed with laboratory equipment. Two cylindrical brass filtration canisters extend from the cheeks at 45-degree angles, connected to the central face cup via ribbed rubber hoses. A small pressure gauge sits on the left cheek showing the needle at "ADEQUATE." The face cup is made of vulcanite (hard rubber) with a Blackwater wax seal embossed on the brow. Slight green patina on the canisters.

---

### `lead_lined_gauntlets`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/lead_lined_gauntlets.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** Heavy-duty industrial gauntlets with visible lead plating over a canvas/leather underglove. The lead plates are rectangular sections, slightly dull-grey and visibly dense, bolted at the knuckles and back of hand. The fingertips are capped with a slightly darker rubber. Acid burn marks (darkened patches, slight corrosion) are visible on the back plates from prior use. A House Blackwater stamp is pressed into the lead near the wrist.

---

### `lead_soled_waders`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/lead_soled_waders.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** Knee-high rubber/oilskin waders in dark grey-green, thick and utilitarian. The soles are dramatically heavy — thick lead plates bolted to the bottom of each boot with large hex bolts. Waterline stains and dark sump residue cover the lower third. The top of the boots fold over and are tied with thick cord. A small flooded-corridor warning placard (Blackwater design language) is riveted near the ankle.

---

### `blackwater_dredging_hook`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/blackwater_dredging_hook.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A long-handled salvage hook approximately 70 cm total. The handle is wrapped in weathered leather cord over an iron rod. The hook tip is a heavy, barbed iron crescent, the outer curve engraved with the Blackwater House crest (a stylised wave over a gear) and the motto *"What sinks, surfaces."* The joint between handle and hook is a brass ferrule stamped with manufacture marks. Light rust at the hook tip, polished where it contacts surfaces.

---

### `blackwater_bilge_charm`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/blackwater_bilge_charm.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A knotted maritime charm roughly 15 cm hanging length. Made from tarred hemp rope in elaborate sailor's knots, threaded with: three verdigris-covered copper coins, a tiny wax-sealed pitch vial (black liquid visible through the glass), and three iron ship's nails. Everything is slightly grime-coated and salt-stained. A red thread weaves through the knots as a protective charm marking.

## The Grand Archive

### `archive_scholars_mortarboard`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/archive_scholars_mortarboard.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A formal academic mortarboard (flat-cap graduation hat) in dark ink-blue velvet, slightly worn at the corners. The flat top is stamped with the Grand Archive's cartographic seal in gold leaf: a compass rose overlaid with an open book. The tassel is woven from thin strips of preserved map-paper rather than thread, hanging slightly stiff. A small quill-and-compass emblem is pinned near the base of the crown.

---

### `archive_curators_gloves`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/archive_curators_gloves.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** Pristine white cotton gloves, institutional in cut, with a slight silk sheen. The backs feature the Archive's classification stamp in pale blue: a magnifying glass over a scroll. The fingertips are reinforced with tiny loops of preservation thread. The cuffs extend slightly past the wrist. Despite their purity, a single rust-coloured stain (artifact residue) is visible on the left palm — clearly impossible to fully launder away.

---

### `archive_soft_soled_shoes`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/archive_soft_soled_shoes.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** Low-heeled Oxford-style inspection shoes in dark tan leather, with linen canvas uppers for silence. The soles are thin and worn smooth from decades of use, showing the leather's grain. Resoled multiple times — the heel has a slightly different leather shade than the sole. The Archive's field researcher serial number is stamped in fading ink on the insole. Simple brass buckles at the ankle strap.

---

### `archive_classification_rod`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/archive_classification_rod.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A telescoping 3-section rod, collapsed to ~40 cm, extending to ~120 cm. The outer section is polished ivory, the inner sections are brass with fine engraved measurement markings. The tip is a small blue-glowing crystal set in a claw mount — the classification indicator that reacts to age. The handle end has an ebonite (black rubber) grip and a brass push-button for extension. The Archive's taxonomy classification numbers are laser-etched near the handle.

---

### `archive_specimen_jar`
**Sprite path:** `ASSETS/ITEMS/ARTIFACTS/archive_specimen_jar.png` / `_full.png`
**Visual brief:** A wide-mouth glass specimen jar approximately 12 cm tall with a rubber-gasketed brass screw lid. Inside, floating in amber-coloured preservative solution, is a partially obscured specimen: the visible portion shows something between a crustacean and a root system, the rest obscured by deliberate frosting of the lower glass. The lid has a wax seal embossed with the Archive's cartographic seal and a handwritten label (illegible) in period ink. The jar exterior is slightly dusty, with a faded printed Archive catalogue sticker.
