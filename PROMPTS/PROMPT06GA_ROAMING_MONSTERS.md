Please implement a comprehensive "Roaming Monsters" and "Noise Ecology" system for the "Hex Maze" game. These entities should be introduced starting from Depth 3. Ensure the implementation adheres to the existing Vanilla JS architecture (no build steps, ES2020) and integrates smoothly with the `GameLoop` and off-screen canvas rendering.

### 1. Expanded Sensory & Noise Ecology
Implement a dynamic stealth and detection system based on a global "Noise Event" architecture.
- **Hearing (Sound Waves & Events):**
  - Noise is not just generated by movement. Create a system where various actions emit "Noise Events" with specific radii and intensities:
    - *Movement:* Low continuous noise. (Standing completely still generates ZERO noise).
    - *Resource Pickup:* Brief, medium-radius noise (clinking coins/materials).
    - *Environmental Hazards:* Crumbling Floors collapsing, Cave-ins being cleared, or Lava Vents erupting generate high-radius noise.
    - *Building:* Placing a barricade generates a loud noise burst.
  - Sound travels through the maze. Walls heavily muffle the sound radius/intensity but do not block it entirely.
- **Sight (Vision Cones):**
  - Monsters have directional sight cones (e.g., 60-90 degrees, extending 3-5 hexes).
  - Sight requires a direct Line of Sight (LoS). Walls completely block vision. Use a hex-grid-adapted raycasting algorithm to check for wall intersections.

### 2. AI States & Monster Variants
Implement a state machine (Idle, Patrol, Investigate, Chase) and create distinct monster archetypes to ensure gameplay variance:
- **The Patroller:** Follows a fixed path between nodes. Has a long, narrow sight cone but poor hearing.
- **The Lurker:** Remains stationary near chokepoints or loot. Has almost no sight cone but extremely high hearing sensitivity. Wakes up and investigates only if a noise event breaches its threshold.
- **The Hunter:** Slowly wanders randomly but has a wide sight cone and balanced hearing. Actively chases the player until they break LoS and remain completely silent.

### 3. Monster Neutralization & Loot Drops
Since the player has no direct attacks, implement "Environmental Combat".
- **Neutralization:** Monsters can be defeated if they are lured into deadly environmental hazards (e.g., falling into a Crumbling Floor void, being caught in a lava geyser, or being crushed by a collapsing Cave-in). 
- **Dynamic Loot Drops:** Defeated monsters drop loot (Barricade Materials, Gold, or rarely, Artifacts). 
  - The drop amount and rarity must scale with the monster's difficulty and type. For instance, defeating a highly sensitive Lurker drops more valuable resources than a basic Patroller.
  - The loot spawns on the hex where the monster was defeated (or the nearest safe adjacent hex if they fell into a void).

### 4. Impact & Player Penalty Mechanics
When a monster catches (touches) the player, it triggers an effect based on the Depth:
- **Depth 3 (Scattering / Thief Mechanic):** The monster steals a portion of the player's gathered in-run currency. 
  - Implement a **Scattering System:** The stolen currency is forcefully ejected from the player, landing on nearby walkable, unblocked hex cells (1-2 tiles away). The player can pick these back up if they risk maneuvering around the monster.
- **Depth 4+ (The Brute Mechanic):** Touching a monster heavily penalizes the player by instantly subtracting a significant chunk of time from the Nemesis Attack Timer (accelerating Phase C) or directly deducting XP.

### 5. Rendering & Visualization
Provide clear visual feedback for these mechanics in `hex-renderer.js` or the main `GameLoop`:
- **Sight Cones:** Render the vision cones as a faint, semi-transparent overlay (e.g., a dim red gradient) clipped by walls.
- **Noise Ripples:** When any Noise Event occurs (movement, breaking floors, picking up loot), briefly render expanding, faint circular ripples to show the player the exact reach of the sound.
- **Loot Animations:** Ensure scattered loot from collisions or monster drops features a brief bounce/toss animation before resting on the grid.
